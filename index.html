FAQ & PoliciesEverything you need before you ride

Booking

How do summer rentals work?

During June, July and August we rent by the week, Saturday to Saturday. You can book one week or several back to back.

Can I rent for just a few days?

Yes, outside of summer. In spring and fall you can pick any start and end dates when you book online.

How much is due when I book?

A $150 deposit holds your cart. The rest of your balance is due at pickup.

Can I book by phone instead?

Of course. Call (704) 779-0263 and you'll talk directly to the owner.

The carts

How far can I go on a charge?

About 40 miles on one charge with our upgraded lithium batteries, versus roughly 20 for a standard cart. Plug it in each night and you're set for the next day. Please keep the battery above 30% while driving.

How many people fit?

Choose a 4-seater or a 6-seater. Our 6-seaters have plenty of room for the family plus chairs, a cooler, and even the dogs.

Do I have to pay for parking?

No. Our carts are registered with the Town of Oak Island, which means free parking for you.

Policies

Who can drive?

Only drivers with a valid driver's license may operate the cart, and all Town of Oak Island golf cart rules apply.

Where do I pick up and drop off?

6016 E Oak Island Dr, Unit B, Oak Island, NC 28465. We'll confirm your pickup and drop-off times before your trip.

What if something goes wrong with the cart?

Call us right away at (704) 779-0263. We're a small local business and we'll take care of you.
